Privacy policy — Kongsro

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Kongsro is a Chrome extension that hides selected news teasers and articles on Norwegian news sites based on local keyword rules.

Data

  • Kongsro does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.
  • Page content is processed only in your browser to decide what to hide.
  • Settings (on/off and per-site toggles) are stored in Chrome’s sync storage on your Google account devices, managed by Chrome — not by a Kongsro server.
  • Kongsro has no accounts, no analytics, and no remote rule updates.

Permissions

  • storage — save your filter preferences.
  • Host access (vg.no, dagbladet.no, aftenposten.no, nrk.no, tv2.no, nettavisen.no, e24.no, abcnyheter.no, dagsavisen.no) — run the filter on those sites only.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub or use the support email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
